## Summary

**The problem.** The `reasoning_field` feature's learning machinery is structurally dead and its request-serving half is partly suppressed. Measured over the full trace window 2026-10-16T14:59Z → 2026-10-21T15:08Z (59,559 entries, 17,738 chat-mode requests), all episodes on `opencode-go-chat` + `deepseek-v4.1-flash` (~0.2%):

- **The complaint is intermittent and field-independent.** Across 26 episodes: the `reasoning` candidate recovered 5 / complained 9 (plus one non-complaint stop); the `none` candidate (no echo at all) recovered 5 / complained 9. No field rule fits that; retries behave as re-rolls recovering roughly half the time. The persisted `reasoning_content` selection (unchanged since 2026-10-12) is not the problem, but neither does anything else fix it.
- **The walk engaged in the reported incident** — both failing requests entered three times and exhausted — and the client received the raw 400 (`retries: 0`; the walk emits no events). Five of six tester launches died on this.
- **Two requests were silently denied** the walk by the feature's process-global non-blocking `reasoning_retry_lock`, proven by exact event overlap (`631d759a` inside `26fe0d46`'s 12:28:24–34 window; `a726831b` inside `9f441f0e`'s 12:12:23–34); both sessions' request streams end at the 400.
- **Four requests died on gate-false bodies** (no thinking to echo) where the walk is skipped by design — correctly, since candidates cannot vary anything, but no retry happened at all.
- **The ladder, full-walk counter, and latch cannot fire under concurrency**: their "consecutive" conditions are reset by any feature-active success for the shared per-key state. Zero `compatibility_latched` events in the window; the ladder has produced no switch since 2026-10-12.

**The change — decouple learning from rescue.**

```
top-level chat request
  ├─ attempt 1 (selection = persisted entry, or "none")
  │    ├─ 2xx                → credit +1 (cap S_MAX)                    → serve
  │    ├─ matched complaint  → credit −1; at 0 (edge, one lock hold):   → serve attempt 1's
  │    │                       advance the entry one step, credit := S_SW 400 / rescue below
  │    └─ other failure      → no credit effect                         → serve
  └─ rescue (any matched complaint, gate-free, LOCK-FREE, key-free):
       up to K=2 passes, candidates cycled from the sent selection,
       original client body, serial 429/503-disabled pass,
       per-attempt `reasoning_rescue` event;
       2xx → serve that pass · non-complaint or exhausted → serve attempt 1's error
  gate-false complaints: rescue only — never learning evidence
  (the scorekeeping block sits BELOW the `suppress_compat or active_feature
   is None or _is_count_tokens` guard — re-entries and response mode
   never reach it)
```

**Key decisions.**
- **D1 — learning is a saturating credit, fed by attempt-1 outcomes only.** +1 per gate-true 2xx (cap `S_MAX`), −1 per gate-true matched complaint; at 0 the selection advances one step in the cycle `none → reasoning_content → reasoning → none` (existing learned/switched/delisted events; `none` delists) and the credit becomes `S_SW`. The switch is **edge-triggered and atomic**: the decrement, the `before > 0 and after == 0` decision, and the `S_SW` reset happen in one `state.lock` hold, so one drain yields exactly one advance; the advance re-reads the entry's **current** selection under the lock (never the request's stale sent value) and moves one step from that. Evidence is the **upstream status**: a chat-mode post-2xx synthesized failure (unfinished-turn synthesis, size-cap truncation, mid-stream death) still credits +1 — accepted bias, bounded by the refill margin. In-memory only (restart clears — fail-safe: evidence restarts from the conservative `S_SW`).
- **D2 — a selection arrives with `S_SW` credit** (first sight, post-switch, post-restart alike): "we do not easily assume we found the correct configuration". Sustained breakage therefore switches within ~`S_SW` complaint episodes and keeps cycling until successes refill; flakiness at ~0.2% with dense successes never drains it.
- **D3 — the rescue is decoupled from learning and unlocked.** Every matched complaint gets up to K=2 re-rolls regardless of the gate; rescue outcomes teach nothing; the candidate cycle is retained because it is free under flakiness and is the only thing that serves the *systematic* case on attempt 2 instead of after ~S_SW dead requests. For gate-false bodies every rescue pass is byte-identical to attempt 1 (no thinking block → the transform emits no echo for any candidate), so the cycle there is a pure re-roll; for unparseable bodies the same holds (the transform never ran).
- **D4 — the ladder, full-walk counter, latch, and `reasoning_retry_lock` are deleted.** The lock's correctness role vanished with D1 (no shared state in the rescue). Its load role does not transfer: before, at most one walk was in flight process-wide; after, rescues are unbounded in concurrency and the ~3× amplification persists as long as the upstream keeps complaining — switch cadence bounds *switching*, not load. That exposure is accepted knowingly: the bounds are the operator escape hatches (`stop`, `/admin/shutdown`) plus a future evidence-gated governor, and a silent denial whose failure mode is a dead session is not an acceptable load policy.
- **D5 — the two thresholds are operator policy in `models.json`** (`reasoning_field_switch: {saturation_max: 10, saturation_on_switch: 3}`), validated like `disable_retry_claude_count_token` (structural in the loader, semantic in `validate_config`, defaults when absent), hot-applied on admin `reload`, read per request from the config snapshot.
- **D6 — no artificial inter-attempt delay**: the passes' own ~3 s latency is the spacing; the capture-and-replay evidence (Guidance for Planner) re-opens this if recovery shows a time pattern.
- **D7 — per-attempt `reasoning_rescue` events** carry the story the trace could not tell; the `retries` entry field keeps its 429/503-only meaning.

**Alternatives rejected.** Keep the walk+ladder and merely raise the budget (leaves both silent no-op paths and the dead learning intact); single-flight via semaphore/per-key lock (per-key does not even fix the observed collisions — the colliding requests share the key); keep a latch as a give-up valve (its state is unreachable under concurrency and forces a restart to recover); escalate the complaint to a client-retryable 5xx (misstates the upstream status; re-openable via the replay evidence); environment variables instead of JSON (JSON is the established policy surface, see D5); per-provider knobs (no operator need and no measurement to set them).

**Out of scope.** The upstream's root cause (lives behind opencode's gateway; the replay evidence is the report to them); any change to `context_management`; changing the `retries` trace field's semantics; the retry-amplification governor; the sibling repo's launcher retry.
